Privacy Policy

Budget Planner by SVSystem
Effective date: November 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Budget Planner by SVSystem (“the App”) handles information when you use it.

1. Information you enter

The App lets you enter budget amounts, income and expense transactions, categories, descriptions and dates. This information is stored locally on your device using on-device app storage. The App does not require you to create an account and the App's budgeting feature does not send these entries to an SVSystem server.

2. Advertising and third-party services

The App uses Google AdMob to request and display advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may collect or receive information such as device identifiers, IP address, app interactions, diagnostics and advertising-related information, as described in Google's policies, to provide, measure and improve advertising, prevent fraud and comply with legal requirements. The information processed by Google is subject to Google's terms and privacy disclosures.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

3. Data sharing

SVSystem does not sell your locally stored budget entries. Budget entries are not transmitted to SVSystem by the App. Information may be processed by Google through the advertising functionality as explained above. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect rights, safety and security.

4. Data retention and deletion

Your budget entries remain in the App's local storage until you delete them, clear the App's storage or uninstall the App. Uninstalling the App generally removes its locally stored data. Advertising-related data handled by Google is subject to Google's retention and deletion practices.

5. Children's privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and is not designed specifically for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a third-party service, please contact us and review Google's privacy controls.

6. Security

The App stores budgeting information on your device. Please use your device's security features and do not share access to your device with people you do not trust. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

7. Your choices

You can remove individual transactions in the App, clear its storage or uninstall it. You can manage advertising personalization and privacy settings through your Android device and Google's available controls.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App or its services change. The updated policy will be published at this URL with a revised effective date.
